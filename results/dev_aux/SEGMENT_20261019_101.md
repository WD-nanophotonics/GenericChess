## Interpretation and limits

- At this dose, self-play removes the mismatch between the learner's coefficients and the foe's. It does not show a utility advantage over a fixed-foe learner, and it costs feedback density per paid node. Fixednu stays a competitive control.
- This does not refute self-play in general: the panel is finite, seeds disagree, and the result is specific to standard Chess.
- The finite-horizon and replanning mismatch, and the lack of an exact policy critic, still apply to both methods.
- The weak 1.25M→2.5M gain suggests the plain-TD utility gain is close to saturating against these two foes under D2 search. Whether a much larger dose or a different target would change this is still open.
- Seed 27182 has a pooled contrast of exactly 0 in several cells. Those are sums of opposite-sign 8-ply and 64-ply cells, not identical games (see by_seed in summary.json).

# Task 2600 — task2592 registered-root identity audit

**Question.** Did the fixed task2592 held-out family lists yield unique full-history EVAL roots and avoid every root actually used by that rule's TRAIN games?

**Result.** All 8 registered EVAL histories were unique within each of the four rule seeds. The actual TRAIN/EVAL overlap is entirely concentrated in rule 364004: all 8 of its EVAL roots also occur as a recorded training initial history. Seeds 364001, 364002 and 364003 had 0/8 such overlaps. The 8/8 overlap for 364004 expands to 256 paired slots (8 roots × 4 learner seeds × 2 roles × 2 endpoints × 2 foes), exactly matching the already published task2592 aggregate `eval_slots_with_actual_TRAIN_root=256` in `eval/summary.json`. Thus this audit stratifies and independently confirms an existing aggregate warning; it is not a newly discovered W/L failure.

**Why this matters.** The task2592 selector scanned family IDs and appended a candidate when its root was absent from a nominal TRAIN-family set, but it did not maintain an EVAL seen-history set. The selected 8 roots happened to be unique in all four rules, but the code did not guarantee that property. The selector also compared against the registered nominal range, while this audit compared the fixed EVAL roots against actual `initial_record` histories in the completed training games. This distinction localizes the known 256 overlapping slots to one rule. Existing task2592 analysis already disclosed the aggregate overlap; no outcome interpretation is revised here.

**Method.** The audit verified all pinned SHA-256 inputs from `design-v3.json`, checked registered family IDs against the stored eval plan, reconstructed only those exact four-ply roots, and compared canonical full histories to TRAIN `initial_record` rows. It did not read W/L outcomes, scores, model weights or evaluation-game logs; it did not train, regenerate rules or select replacement roots. Exact output is in `root-audit-v2.json`. The earlier `root-audit.json` is preserved with a wrong design-hash field and is superseded only for that metadata by v2. Three preflight errors (two hand-transcribed SHA pins and one design-status assertion) are preserved as `preflight-failure*.json`; each stopped before root-history data processing. The original designs remain intact.

**Implication.** A future preregistration should construct EVAL records from a bounded predeclared candidate stream, check EVAL uniqueness explicitly, and compare each canonical EVAL history against the actual frozen TRAIN root manifest before fitness. Record the conditional sampling target and all rejections. This does not change task2592 or task2595 results, and does not authorize reusing task2595 seed 364005 or its failed EVAL families.

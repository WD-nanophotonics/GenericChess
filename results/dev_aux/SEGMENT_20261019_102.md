## Limits

- **UNKNOWN rises with training:** initial 9, 100k 23, 200k 34, 400k 35, OldRulesMean 20. Trained models reach the H128 horizon more often, which widens the bounds; the H choice can affect the results.
- **State collision:** 16 of 32 EVAL (rule, family) root records also occurred as TRAIN roots. The rules share one layout, and random 4-ply prefixes on 4×4 collide. DEV_AUX96 had the same issue.
- **POST-HOC sensitivity** (designed after fitness, not registered; task2586/eval/exposure-sensitivity.json). On the 1280 non-exposed slots, the conclusions are unchanged:
  - Fixed@100k − initial: [+.254, +.410]
  - Fixed@400k − initial: [+.133, +.359]
  - OldRulesMean − initial: [+.117, +.258]
  - Fixed@400k − OldRulesMean: [−.109, +.227]
- **Scope:** four rules from one generator configuration (4×4, the same piece types) is a small slice of the generated domain. These are finite paired bounds, with no population, all-rule or stable-price claim. The foes were already exposed in DEV_AUX96.

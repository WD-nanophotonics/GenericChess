## Interpretation and limits

- Averaged old-rule prices are not a reliable prior for new rules from the same generator. On one panel they helped; on another they were neutral. As a starting point they also slowed learning at equal dose.
- One possible mechanism, not tested: TD from a mid-range warm start makes smaller or misdirected updates. Another: averaging over rules mixes signs per channel.
- Scope: 4 + 4 rules from one generator configuration; repeated roots; foes exposed. No population or all-rule claim.
- Combined with DEV_AUX102: per-rule TD learning from random initialization is the robust result across 8 NEW rules (+.17 to +.19 by 100k). Rule-independent price reuse is not.

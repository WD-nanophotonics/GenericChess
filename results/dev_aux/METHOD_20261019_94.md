# Candidate search depth, training signal and actual rule utility

Two prospective own-lane studies reuse the published93 continuous learners and saved endpoints. No new architecture, prover, Native optimizer, product/default/UI change or additional Agent is introduced. Source and evidence remain on sidemission. Scientific status OPEN.

The first study (task2552) holds all93 prices fixed, uses all4 TRAIN-exposed generated4x4 rules, all4 random initializations, both candidate roles and two fixed opponents. Each opponent always searches D2. Candidate search alternates D2 and D3 in a frozen768-slot matrix on two new PRNG prefix recipes per rule. Roots, tie-order path seed, initial models, opponent tables and per-query16384-node/2s ceiling are identical across depth treatments. The actual work differs and is paid. This removes the previous93 root-panel/opponent-depth confound, while remaining a resource-conditioned deployed-policy comparison, not equal CPU or a universal depth theorem.

The second study (task2555/2556), registered before new TRAIN or outcome-dependent choices, reuses the exact6free TD and mean bound-aware TreeStrap code, all4 source rules/initials, original TRAIN prefix and ordering recipes, eta.05/lambda.95 and200000 ALL-paid nodes per arm. Only candidate TRAIN search changes to D3/16384 ceiling; the uniform TRAIN opponent remains D2/4096. All32 new endpoints, all32 originalD2 endpoints and16 common initial policies enter a separately frozen640-slot D3 deployment matrix. Both deployment opponents stay D2, with new261001/261002 prefix recipes. Training changes visitation, query count, internal interval mix and update opportunities, not just a scalar target. Equal paid nodes and the same eta do not imply equal wall/CPU time, effective step size or pure information ablation.

Material representation is signed board-current and hand-base P/A/X counts. All6 coefficients are free after initialization: boardP starts at1 but is not pinned. Tanh scale10 is fixed. No human prices, sign flip, clipping, posthoc normalization or best-seed/rule/root choice is deployed. Actual promotion, drops and relevant Session history are retained by full rules. These four shared-channel generated rules are a scope, not a redefinition of the entire generated domain.

TD uses candidate-clock observations with theta frozen per episode, valid white-coordinate value/gradient, reverse lambda credit and actual terminal endpoint where reached. Unknown endpoints add no invented terminal target. Mean TreeStrap updates only after complete candidate queries, averaging one-sided transformed squared losses over returned nonterminal interior nodes. Upper/lower bounds remain one-sided. A partial root contributes no samples. The selected action was computed before its query update and is then executed. This is an operational variant, not a literal historical TreeStrap replication.

Only actual rule terminals yield W/L/D utility. UNKNOWN and missing planned slots carry[-1,1], not a material-count verdict or draw. Paired contrasts average over all registered rule/initial/family/role cells, combining repeated policy coefficients before bounding. Primary within-depth learned-minus-initial contrasts and their D3-minus-D2 interactions are frozen; direct TreeStrap-minus-TD comparisons are secondary. Newtraining comparisons freeze D3TD-D2TD, D3Tree-D2Tree, both new initial gains and direct method controls. All adverse strata are retained. Three-thousand crossed resamples of4 rules/4 initial seeds/2 prefix families are descriptive envelopes, not guaranteed population intervals, equivalence or adjusted multiple-test claims. Rule and trajectory repetitions are not independent games.

Eight full roots per panel are replayed from the prescribed random legal prefix, including all overlap/terminal cases. New PRNG numbers do not imply unseen complete states; overlap with93 TRAIN is recorded. Old incomplete D3 slots and caps are never resumed. New original caps are1800s for the matched matrix and2700s+900s for new TRAIN/deployment. The first matrix's initialization seed error occurred before any search; exact originals are saved and its repair time consumes the original wall clock.

Independent audits replay every new query and final typed record, verify actor-specific search depth, fixed opponent weights, total paid nodes, complete/incomplete receipts, actual outcome and signed candidate reward. Every first divergent paired action must be the candidate's. Existing43 learner/search controls are supplemented by96 prospectively selected unpruned D2/D3 root/action/PV checks and32 new TRAIN D3 checks under separate original budgets. They do not prove every search or provide an external rules authority. Full new TRAIN scalar updates, endpoint chains and all per-query mean losses are reconstructed independently. Per-arm exact observed/active-credit feature ranks, saturation and own-unit loss bins diagnose usable signal; rank6 or coefficient concentration alone does not establish identified useful prices.

DeepSeek's saved static invariance/geometry work is separate and is not merged or rerun. Its useful caution about material-dependent judges is respected: deeper material scores are search outputs, not terminal quality labels. PRIMARY's saved warning that equal roots need not preserve internal TreeStrap interval credit is noted; no Native trace/window optimization is adopted. Both roles remain read-only here. Raw Slack originals are stored privately before interpretation; no routine acknowledgments or additional workers.

The exact new capsule will pin executed source, rules, raw query/game/update journals, source93 inputs needed for signal rechecks and the original audits. A fresh ignored extraction parks original derived files before regenerating them under Python-S/empty PYTHONPATH and checks real source locations. Historical capsules retain their hashes; not all previous experiments are rerun. Final reports, aggregate delivery/fresh receipts and remote verification are separate metadata to avoid circular self-reference. Actual costs distinguish visited nodes, search receipts, wall time and unmeasured engineering/transport/token overhead.

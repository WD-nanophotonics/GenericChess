## Implemented method and primary-source boundary

The primary candidate is episode-frozen TDLeaf(lambda=.95): save the selected
PV leaf's white-oriented prediction and derivative, accumulate discounted TD
credit, then update once at the actual episode boundary. Pawn stays1; the other
four Chess coefficients begin as seeded uniform random draws [.5,1.5]. Prediction
is tanh(material/10). No human value ordering, SEE, material move ordering,
nullmove/futility pruning, opening book, solver or table reward is used. Legal
moves are seed-shuffled and best changes only on STRICT improvement; an equal
alpha-beta bound never replaces a better root action. Actual claims of available
threefold/50-move draws are a predeclared common policy via python-chess1.11.2;
full legal move histories are retained. Unfinished games are U, never draw.

The same small Model/alpha-beta module supports the online TD approximation and
a bound-aware TreeStrap control. TreeStrap applies frozen one-sided interval
losses, averaged across samples, after transforming scores. It is explicitly a
variant, not the original linear/summed algorithm. Full GameSession adapter ran
four actual transitions with separate board-current/hand-base features; this is
interface feasibility, not a generic-domain learning result. No more generic
capability engineering is required before pursuing the learning example.

Primary sources checked:
- [Baxter et al., Figure3/TDLeaf](https://arxiv.org/pdf/cs/9901002): PV-leaf
  gradients and episode TD sum inform the primary update. Its headline KnightCap
  experiment started with expert material values and online opponents; our
  random material-only self-play is a different test. Half-move sampling,
  beta=.1, eta=.05, D2/q0 and shuffled strict ties are stated deviations.
- [Veness et al., Algorithms1/2](https://cgi.cse.unsw.edu.au/~blair/pubs/2009VenessSilverUtherBlairNIPS.pdf):
  one-sided alpha-beta bound losses and independent training search priors.
  Their1812-feature/qsearch system is not our four-weight reproduction target.
- [Beal-Smith, IJCAI1999](https://www.ijcai.org/Proceedings/99-1/Papers/081.pdf):
  temporal coherence adapts per-weight rates; it is not exploration. The optional
  online control omits adaptive prediction decay.
- [Beal-Smith1997](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.3233/ICG-1997-20302):
  publisher abstract read; full text not obtained. No faithful replication claim.

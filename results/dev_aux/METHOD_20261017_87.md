# Primary-method alignment — segment87

The active update is episode-frozen TDLeaf(lambda=.95), with gradients through
the selected principal-variation leaf, pawn fixed to1 and tanh(material/10).
This follows the structure of equation4 and the search-leaf construction in
[TDLeaf(lambda), Baxter/Tridgell/Weaver1999](https://arxiv.org/pdf/cs/9901001).
A terminal search forecast has zero local gradient but preserves earlier
eligibility until the ACTUAL outcome. A censored game omits its final terminal
delta, retaining completed bootstrap transitions. These are explicit engineering
choices; ties use coefficient-independent random order and the chosen PV branch.
No mathematical convergence is claimed for this nonlinear on-policy scheme.

[TreeStrap, Veness etal2009](https://cgi.cse.unsw.edu.au/~blair/pubs/2009VenessSilverUtherBlairNIPS.pdf)
uses search-derived training and describes capture/check-evasion quiescence
without coefficient-informed pruning. It motivates a tactical-target comparison,
not treating its sample counts,1812features or reported strength as our result.
Our full-capture/evasion pilot exhausted its original240s cap. Its incomplete
second phase remains incomplete. The separate q2 approximation allows explicitly
counted NONCHECK static cutoffs even when captures remain; all check evasions
continue. It is neither the paper's full quiescence nor an exact WDL label.

Beal/Smith1997's [publisher abstract](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.3233/ICG-1997-20302)
supports material learning via TD/minimax, but its full method was not obtained.
The author's public thesis URL returned no usable text through the current web
reader. Therefore this study is an explicit TDLeaf-style implementation, NOT a
faithful1997replication. [Beal/Smith2000](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0020-0255(99)00093-6)
abstract distinguishes temporal coherence/prediction decay; our retained online
TC control does not reproduce its full adaptation. No human price is fitted.

The comparison holds total training NODE work fixed, not wall time, episode
count or data distribution. Adding q2 changes selfplay actions and PV targets
simultaneously. All trained sets deploy under the SAMEplainD2 search against the
same uniform foe. The estimand is complete training-regime usefulness for that
player, not pure target accuracy or the strength of q-enabled deployment.
One initial baseline per seed/family/role is physically paid once and shared;
direct q2-trained minus plain-trained cancels it. Families, not games/checkpoints,
are the repeated unit; four seeds do not establish universal stability.

repeated across checkpoints and are not reclassified as fresh for each point.
Final strength uses only actual rules W/L/D; U and missing remain bounded and
reported. Training loss is on changing policy-generated data, not a validation
loss. Weight movement, changed PVs or more solver activity are insufficient.

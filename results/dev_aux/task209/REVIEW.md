# Primary-source feasibility review, 2026-10-12

Beal and Smith's1997 publisher abstract reports learning suitable Chess piece values using temporal differences applied to minimax searches, with play performance comparable to familiar reference values. We accessed the abstract, not the restricted full experiment protocol. This supports keeping a TD/minimax route open; it supplies no verified sample count, short-game recipe or claim about our current mature engine. [Publisher abstract](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.3233/ICG-1997-20302)

Coulom's CLOP paper distinguishes optimizing game outcomes from cheaper surrogate criteria and warns that surrogate optimization need not maximize winning probability. It models noisy parameter responses and reuses observations; its assumptions and algorithm differ from our deterministic ridge fits. Our Task207 is a small quadratic response-surface feasibility check, not CLOP replication. [Author's paper](https://www.remi-coulom.fr/CLOP/CLOP.pdf)

Our inference: the current short proxy failures neither refute fixed distribution-averaged material values nor show that mature search cannot learn them. They identify a limited objective/search/distribution combination. Task207 reuses candidate-price observations rather than fitting material counts; it gives mixed root transfer and indefinite fitted Hessians. We will not deploy its extreme predicted maxima as proven prices.

Task208 is a concrete prerequisite check for a cheaper TD backup. All50020 recorded UCI PVs replay legally, but18632/36438 unbounded nonterminal cp records differ from the material at their literal PV endpoint. Task210's four actual endpoint eval readbacks reproduce pure material. A legal PV is not an exported certificate of the selected static leaf or root-value derivative. The source allows TT substitution and qsearch futility-bound updates without searching the corresponding move; that is a possible mechanism, not attribution of these cases. Full-root/history, bound flags and draw claims must remain explicit.

No source supplies new human target labels, an approval gate, permission for long games or a reason to transfer Chess semantics to Shogi. No paper was purchased or access restriction bypassed; the university thesis URL returned no readable document and no experiment details were inferred from it. A scoped TD hypothesis can still be constructed, provided its bootstrap/feature approximation is stated and independently checked.

# Same-engine TD versus paid terminal selection (segment92)

The question is whether search-derived incremental feedback improves material learning per total computation, compared with a specified terminal-WDL coordinate selector. This is the methodological comparison requested by the user. The older prover-centered/WDL-only route remains historical evidence, not a prerequisite. This study does not change product/Core/UI/defaults or PRIMARY's checkout.

Both methods use six raw, signed coefficients: current board P/A/X and base hand A/P/X, tanh scale10, identical initial vectors, the same full GameSession rule authority and plain complete depth2 Alpha-Beta. There are no human prices, SEE, price-dependent move ordering, selective pruning or WIN-prover calls. Four previously exposed generated4x4 promotion/drop rules each retain separate parameters. The arbitrary generated P is not a Chess pawn, so all six coordinates remain free. This is not an all-generated-domain demonstration or literal Beal-Smith replication.

For each rule and all four initializations, each arm pays at most200,000 visited search nodes, including opponent moves, unsuccessful queries, and both current/proposed-price games. The prospective arm order is shuffled without outcomes. Root recipe181000 onward, legal random prefix4, horizon128, node4096/time2s per query and aggregate3600s training cap were frozen before the first query. Neither incomplete search nor game horizon creates a draw label. No shallow fallback, cap extension, best seed or selected checkpoint is used. Whole study forecast75-105minutes is below the user's120-minute consultation threshold; that threshold is distinct from Agent segment duration.

TD freezes its weights throughout each game. It observes only its own candidate-turn search predictions/PV gradients; the fixed uniform opponent's searches consume budget but supply no learner targets or gradients. The next candidate decision includes the intervening opponent action as part of the transition. Gamma1 and lambda.95 apply per candidate decision, eta.05; this is a new clock, not the earlier every-halfply selfplay clock. Predictions, gradients and actual terminal targets remain white-oriented even when the candidate is black. Unknown endings omit the invented final target while retaining legitimate preceding temporal differences. Terminal PV gradients are zero, but earlier eligibility may still receive a real terminal error. The same TDLeafEpisode/FreeModel/TrainingSearch code is reused.

WDL proposes a signed additive change of.25 to one coordinate. All12 coordinate/sign possibilities are permuted prospectively per cycle and initialization seed. Each proposal pays current and proposed-price play on four fresh root families and both roles (16 games per full block), with shared action-order seeds and balanced treatment execution order. Each actual terminal supplies candidate utility white z or black -z. Unknown or missing cells contribute[-1,1]. Accept only if the paired proposal-minus-current lower bound is strictly positive; ties retain current weights. Every new block pays the baseline again; there are no free cached counterfactuals. A partially affordable block preserves its actual games and missing cells, applies the same worst-case rule, and never resets its pool.

This strict lower bound describes the finite training panel, not a statistical confidence interval. It can be very conservative and its small panel can still overfit. A negative result only concerns this step/order/selector and budget; it cannot rule out terminal learning or coordinate optimization generally. TD and WDL differ in update rules, sample exposure and effective step size. Thus the experiment compares operational regimes under shared model/search/opponent/node ceilings, not a pure ablation of information alone. Per-arm wall and game/update counts are measured as consequences, not assumed equal. Ordinary concurrent load is a wall-time interpretation limit.

Final evaluation was also registered before queries: all4rules x4initializations x8 new families191001..191008 x2roles x(initial,TD,WDL) x2 fixed opponents =1536games. The uniform and previous random99031 opponents are fixed; the latter is not assumed stronger. Initial games are paid once per shared panel cell. The direct TD-minus-WDL contrast and both initial controls retain every root/seed/outcome/UNKNOWN/missing cell. New PRNG families are not a promise of unseen full states; current TRAIN overlap and already terminal roots are audited without filtering. No old151001..8 fitness is used for selection/refitting.

Independent checks reconstruct every paid query's full typed history, candidate-only TD credit by reverse future-error convolution, black candidate utility, the prospective proposal sequence, all strict bound decisions, root pairing and node totals. A separate unpruned depth2 implementation checks the first complete candidate query at0 and100,000 paid nodes in every arm (64 planned), including optimal committed typed child and PV leaf witness. This is independent search arithmetic over the same Core authority, not an independent rules engine or all-query proof. The eight existing learning/search/rule targets passed38 tests. Fresh extraction will recompute actual audits rather than merely read sealed outputs; its result must be reported from execution.

The classical TDLeaf reference is [Baxter, Tridgell and Weaver](https://arxiv.org/pdf/cs/9901001). Existing TreeStrap variants were compared in segment88; this study's WDL contrast fills a different gap. Search targets remain approximate training feedback, and actual rule outcomes remain strength validation. Neither lower training loss nor changed moves alone establish useful learning or stable universal prices.

The TD root target is the finite-depth minimax value under the candidate evaluator, while the actual intervening opponent uses its own fixed prices. It is therefore search bootstrapping through an approximate value representation, not an unbiased Monte Carlo estimate of that opponent's win probability. Candidate-turn sampling makes the transition/weight version coherent; it does not remove approximation or prove convergence. The final independently registered actual-outcome panel checks usefulness. Equal nodes also do not equal equal games: learned policies can change branching, episode lengths and how often an arm reaches a zero-cost terminal prefix. Those exposure differences must be reported, not silently normalized away.

The six learned numbers are coefficients of this specified evaluator and policy/distribution, not isolated causal values of adding/removing a piece. Board-current and hand-base channels can carry correlated consequences of promotion, exchange and deployment. A signed coefficient, a changed rank or improved actual play cannot by itself establish an intrinsic universal material price. This distinction matters when interpreting apparent convergence: several raw vectors may induce similar decisions or utility, and poorer raw agreement is not automatically failure to learn useful play. Conversely, actual utility improvement alone does not establish stable transferable price ratios. Both claims remain separate.

Feedback-density analysis also distinguishes temporal terms targeting the next nonterminal material PV, the next terminal PV encountered inside search, and the actual game endpoint. A zero gradient at a terminal PV does not erase earlier eligibility. Gross absolute credit is reported before cancellation and is not a fraction of net parameter movement or proof that any source caused strength gains. This checks whether apparent dense learning is merely material self-consistency or also receives rule-terminal anchors; no additional games or labels are introduced.

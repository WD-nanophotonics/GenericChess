## Correct attacker-centric definition

The ambiguous `ordinary_anchor_zone_coverage_by_owner` meaning is retired.
The trajectory schema now records `ordinary_attack_coverage_by_owner` where
the key is attacker owner `a`, the target Anchor owner is `1-a`, and:

```text
zone = target Anchor's zone
attacks = ordinary attacks generated by attacker owner a
coverage = attacks intersect zone
```

Each owner entry includes `attacker_owner`, `target_anchor_owner`,
`target_anchor_square`, `coverage_fraction`,
`target_anchor_itself_attacked`, and the attacked squares. The side-to-move
summary is derived from the entry for the side to move, so its target is the
enemy Anchor.

For a capture of a piece owned by `c`, the state after the move records the
same owner `c`'s attacker-centric coverage against owner `1-c`'s Anchor:
the captured owner/type, whether the captured piece was ordinary,
pre-capture coverage, post-capture coverage, and
`captured_owner_attack_coverage_delta = post - pre`. Non-ordinary captures are
explicitly marked and excluded from the ordinary capture-delta summary.

A direct synthetic Core-position regression places one owner-0 ordinary
attacker so that owner 0 covers a nonzero part of owner 1's Anchor zone while
owner 1 has zero ordinary coverage. It therefore fails if coverage is keyed
by target Anchor owner rather than attacker owner.

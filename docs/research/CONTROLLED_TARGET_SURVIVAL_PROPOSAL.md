# Changed controlled task: designated removal and retained custody

2026-10-04. A prospective approximation question, not an admitted constructor
or permission to rerun any spent uniform-service or local-safe-capture batch.
No new goal/candidate label or human reference is used to set this objective.

Motivation: a search leaf treats a resource as something its owner can choose
to deploy, rather than something a uniform policy happens to use. Ask a finite
task question with explicit information: at most two own turns, starting now,
can a tagged ordinary resource remove a designated existing ordinary enemy
token AND retain own custody through the second enemy reply? Public legal
actions/history only; the controller may prepare, promote or drop first and
capture on the second own turn. The opponent selects replies adversarially.
Both observe public state/actions and the declared identity law, never hidden
physical hand labels. No clairvoyant policy per hidden tagged instance.

Task payoff1 is the JOINT event of designated removal BY THE TAG at least once
and source-tag custody retained at the end of four action plies. Uncompleted
or absorbed ownership-lost worlds have payoff0. Earlier ordinary terminal
endpoints stop the task and use its physical completion/custody event, without
an added WDL bonus or price. Unsupported claims/RESTART/NO_CONTEST remain
unknown; a concrete runner must freeze that treatment and full semantic scope.
This is a resource-control task, not global WDL or a claim that a terminal
loser's surviving physical token has strategic value.

Differences from failed routes are testable. LOCAL_SERVICE_VALIDATION_PROTOCOL
requires an IMMEDIATE board capture, keeps actor/victim under own custody for
ONE reply, excludes held/promoted initial actors and predicts actor counts
after inventory changes. This task admits preparation/drop/promotion and two
own turns, fixes one target identity, ends with source custody, and asks a
controllable completion probability rather than a count-transfer regression.
It does not require target to remain outside enemy custody after being dropped
again; completion is a historical event. That difference must not be hidden.
The old global net-token task instead includes unrelated background gains/
losses; this task's designated event does not. These changes do not guarantee
new values differ or are useful; do not run a renamed one-step restriction.

Conditional on a fixed public action, update the joint identity law and event
flag through actual ordered effects; THEN take expected endpoint payoff. At
our nodes maximize over complete PUBLIC choices, at opponent nodes minimize.
Expectation over exchangeable physical-label assignment occurs before this
choice optimization. max(E payoff) generally differs from E(max payoff).
Capture and survival marginals cannot be multiplied without independence.

For a subproblem with unchanged original action subtrees, adding optional own
actions cannot decrease the max value. Adding opponent actions cannot increase
the min value. Changing checks, opponent actions, goals, history or subtrees
voids this comparison. The uniform-policy quantity need not have either
property: adding a quiet zero-return own action lowers its average. This is
a meaning check, not proof that optimal unit capture equals material strength.

## Smallest next qualification, before game samples

First use independent finite labelled-world trees to test information order,
capture/custody correlation, optional-action monotonicity and interval backup
with unexpanded choices. Do not sample a game coefficient or fit a vector.
Then specify a narrow actual two-turn preparation witness that the old immediate
capture task cannot solve, with real legal transitions and designated physical
target effects. Full target/source joint tracing, budgeted branch certification,
common target/context law and held-mode support remain open.

Static transfer and reserved-use remain separate assumptions. Exact controlled
task values may saturate at1 for many types, be0 under minimax, or cost too much;
those would be substantive failures for the specified law, not a license for
damping, larger horizon, human prices or repeated seed selection.

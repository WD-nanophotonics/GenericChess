## Wider compilation remains open

[Regular Games](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.10593v1) specializes automaton execution
and reachability into C++ functions and restores old values during traversal.
Its automaton-terminal and keeper-equivalence assumptions do not supply this
project's legal-existence/history semantics. The authors mark condition reordering
unsafe; it is not adopted here. This suggests a broader compilation premise
than the deferred Python effect specializations, with translation/callback costs
still unknown; the paper's benchmark does not predict our caller benefit.

[The forward-chaining GDL compiler](https://www.lamsade.dauphine.fr/~cazenave/papers/gadelac.pdf)
separates permanent, persistent and ephemeral facts and stratifies negation.
Our inference is that compiled geometry is reusable, but occupancy/check/history
facts require reversible invalidation. Persistence along a play does not license
cross-sibling reuse. Select measured algorithm scheduling now; this neither
implements a new compiler nor forbids later explicit dependency compilation.

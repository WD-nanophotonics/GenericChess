## Primary literature and scope of adoption

[Schiffel/Thielscher, Fluxplayer2007](https://cdn.aaai.org/AAAI/2007/AAAI07-189.pdf),
pp1193–1195, construct heuristics from goal/terminal formulas and recognized
structures. They acknowledge formula-representation sensitivity and weak
nondescript-goal behavior. Learn the goal-context distinction; a direct fuzzy-GDL
port would require prerequisites not supplied by this RuleSet study. Our DSL
control instead preserves values and changes a search tie, a different mechanism.

[Lai, Giraffe2015](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1509.01549), sections4.1/4.3–4.5/5.3,
uses domain-specific fixed piece slots, a material bootstrap, varied self-play
starts and TDLeaf. Extra promoted pieces lose positional slot detail. Those
representation/data choices are not a complete generic-rule recipe. Learn to
separate representation, sampled experience, value fitting and actual policy
quality; do not import human/engine labels, fixed entity caps or its reported
training cost. Its probability-search proposal is not implemented or evidence
about this repository's backends.

## Question and scope
Can compiled Boolean predicates save useful complete constructor-plus-search
cost while retaining exact Core semantics? One caller-supplied candidate only;
no product default,Native rebuild,game or strength claim. Native full legal action
generation and Core state/history/clocks/adjudication/TT remain unchanged.

## Source evidence and derivation
The previous profile contains38 complete/parity groups. Provider check and
availability are non-nested sibling regions;their combined instrumented CPU share
is10.7965% at D6 and9.0979% at D8. Profiling slows execution about4.4x,so this is
neither uninstrumented removable wall nor a speed forecast. Native availability
already reuses the one state import performed by check. Replacing both Boolean
predicates targets that joint region;replacing check alone shifts its import.

For strictly unconditional leap/move/remove patterns with own-anchor safety,
compile quiet and capture destination masks. Rebuild occupancy and enemy source
attack unions for an immutable physical position. Without rays or other effects,
non-anchor evasion can only capture the last checking source;an anchor landing
must be outside remaining enemy coverage. Prefix/suffix OR supplies the union
without one captured source;XOR is invalid with overlapping attacks. Core forbids
capturing an Anchor even though it contributes geometric coverage. An initial
prototype missed this and failed a retained arbitrary-board counterexample;
corrected before any cost query.13 Core contract tests now pass,including800
arbitrary physical states,directional patterns,overlap,undo,cancel and fallback.
Unsupported rules/states fall back to generic Core. This is not a complete-domain
proof;full-history root/transition comparisons precede the benchmark.

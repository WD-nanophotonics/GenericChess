## A scoped negative result, including captures

For a fixed ordinary Leap attack set S, a sufficient condition is that no two
members of S are orthogonally adjacent. If A checks the defending King k, the
two perpendicular on-board orthogonal neighbours of k are then unattacked by A.
An attacking eight-neighbour King cannot control both without being adjacent to
k. If A occupies one candidate escape, either the other square is available or
A is unprotected and capturable. After capture, A no longer attacks anything.

# Duration union preserves qualified choices

All3124 predeclared signed inventory comparisons were evaluated with exact
rationals in0.157sec, no game transitions or goal queries. The partition is:
2884 stable same-law choices also certified by the outer hull;26 stable
same-law choices lost by the hull;214 with uncertainty inside at least one
duration box. No stable cross-duration disagreement occurred in THIS bounded
[-2,2] domain. This is not proof of agreement for arbitrary inventories.

A constructive hull-loss difference in(Q,R,N,B,P) is(-2,1,2,2,-2): compare
R+2N+2B against2Q+2P. Both model boxes certify the first inventory; their
outer hull fails. Exact lower margins are positive within each law (about
0.05618 fixed-half,0.01280 mixture), but about-0.04505 in the hull. The hull
combines low R/N/B from one duration with high P from the other; those
coefficient combinations are outside the declared two-box union. Neither
side exceeds30 tokens. These are typed inventory fixtures, not asserted legal
sibling positions, and no utility label was obtained.

`contact_duration_union.py` therefore checks the two COMPLETE child certificates
separately and returns a choice only if both are stable with the SAME ID. It
distinguishes incomplete input, within-model uncertainty, duration disagreement
and union stability. The old `both` hull interface and every frozen experiment
remain unchanged. Use this research adapter where the intended uncertainty
set really is these two hypotheses; do not confuse it with a continuum of
undeclared duration distributions or with a search/strategic validation pass.

The adapter adds no model query, point estimate, source labels, midpoint,
unsupported-child filtering or Core change. Four tests independently replay
the hull-loss witness and failures plus census source integrity. The remaining
214 uncertain comparisons still need tighter construction or explicit
uncertainty, not more use labels chosen to tune a duration.

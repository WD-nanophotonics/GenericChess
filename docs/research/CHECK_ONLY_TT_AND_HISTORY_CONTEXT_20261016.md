# Check-only, per-call TT and history-context reuse

Science is OPEN. Neither check-only nor the private TT-key rewrite is adopted.
The complete generated-rule target still includes promotion, drops, auxiliary
state and relevant history. Product source, prices, APIs, defaults and UI stay
unchanged. This is finite method diagnosis, not Elo, price learning or optimal WDL.

## Decision

Actual forced-evasion-only continuation improves local completion relative to
ordinary q1, but that depth benefit did not establish a useful replacement.
The fresh 27-game comparison against disabled q0 gives 4 WIN, 6 LOSS and
8 UNKNOWN versus control-role 5 WIN, 5 LOSS and 8 UNKNOWN: finite net -2/18,
including one WIN-to-LOSS. A separate fresh fixed-opponent four-cell study gives
the same net +1 for each treatment versus its control. Adding check-only to TT
has net 0, changes outcomes in both directions and costs more on this panel.
Keep disabled q0/TT as a cheap baseline; these small development panels do not
justify a new q default, global dominance or a population-strength claim.

The lookup-tag prototype restores potential cache identities and extra hits,
but saves no nodes in either of two actual reuse controls. It also stays private.
A next narrow depth-demotion counter is prepared, with zero counter builds/calls:
source suggests a later shallow generation can overwrite a deeper matching
entry. That is a falsifiable source hypothesis, not a diagnosed correctness bug
or permission to keep repeating an unchanged failed optimization.

## History-context identity is not useful reuse by itself

Normal persistent q0/TT16 gets 104 calls: 13 exposed parents, exact-root and
legacy modes, cold parent, same-parent warm, adjacent warm and adjacent fresh.
Two actual PV moves produce the next same-side root with complete histories.
Python reconstruction of the actual C fold shows exact-root policy tagging
before recursive history extension gives different adjacent key contexts;
legacy contexts match. This is source reconstruction, not an internal Native
key trace or a semantic rule defect. Exact-root adjacent warm/fresh node counts
match on all 13 roots. Legacy-mode D3 records are node-censored and retain that
limit; they do not establish finite-objective equality or a clean speed contrast.

A separate [lookup-tag patch](data/key_policy_20261016.patch) moves only exact-root
q0 domain separation to lookup/store, preserving full history and leaving q>0's
existing salt route unchanged. Its actual 104-call test matches all 52 complete
exact-root score/action/PV results. Reconstructed adjacent contexts now match;
warm TT hits total 10092 versus fresh 8785. Yet all 13 adjacent node counts
remain identical; measured caller sums 1.124/1.138s are not evidence of a useful
general speedup or a finite-game improvement.

A changed matched-remaining-depth control executes another 156 calls in two
module orders: D4 parent then D2 after two PV moves. All 52 parent groups finish
D4 and both children D2; all warm/fresh scores/actions/PVs and node counts match.
The cross-image complete objectives match too. The key rewrite remains rejected
for adoption. native_tt.c allows matching-entry replacement when depth increases
OR generation advances, so shallow new entries may erase deeper old ones.
That is a source-supported next hypothesis with zero direct counter executions,
not a measured causal explanation. No broader TT rewrite is started.

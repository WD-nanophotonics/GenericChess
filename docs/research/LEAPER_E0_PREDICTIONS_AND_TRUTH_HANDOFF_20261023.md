## A new rule-derived E0 candidate, with a narrow proof obligation

After the policy-feature failure, a genuinely different cheap coordinate is the
parity set of ordinary offsets: all(dx+dy)mod2 equal. This includes both color-
preserving and color-flipping pieces; it is not just empty-board connectivity.
For4x4 K+AagainstbareK, eight-neighbour royal Anchors, exactly one ordinary actor,
plain remove/no hand/drop/promotion/auxiliary effects, and checkmate as the only
scoring terminal, we propose a one-sided mating obstruction when that parity set
has one member. Other goals, stalemate loss, extra pieces, thin boards and other
Anchor geometry are excluded. Capturing A yields a still smaller bare-King case;
no stock or quantity conservation is assumed.

Reasoning for independent review: choose two perpendicular on-board orthogonal
neighbours u,v of the checked King. A single-parity checker attacks squares of
the victim's color, leaving u,v unattacked by it. The attacking King cannot guard
both u,v without being adjacent to the victim. If A occupies one of those escape
squares, protecting A while covering the other requires the same forbidden King
intersection; the victim can capture A or escape. A bare King cannot itself give
a legal royal check. This is an analytic E0 proposal, not an independently
certified global closure or a new exported WDL label. Repetition/clock draws do
not supply a missing checkmate; history rules that award losses would need
separate treatment.

## What the natural four-actor histories establish

The larger natural-history census finds real decision errors that the previous
24-root panel did not expose. Among **898 qualified full histories**, Unit D1
serves a worse-WDL action 40 times. Yet changing positive material ratios in the
declared window cannot improve those choices. They arise from score ties between
good and bad actions. Increasing depth resolves most of them: Unit D2 leaves
six errors and D4 leaves one. Mobility and Static-material priors have the same
WDL results at each of those depths.

These are exposed finite decision comparisons. They are neither population
confidence intervals nor whole-policy game win rates. A better action in a

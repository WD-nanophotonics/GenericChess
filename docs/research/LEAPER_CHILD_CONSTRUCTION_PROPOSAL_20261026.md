## Decision boundary

Any semantic or work/exception contract disagreement means NO-GO pending diagnosis,
not a performance gain. Eligibility requires both arms complete the requested
fixed depth under the frozen call and batch bounds; report the entire fixed slot
denominator, individual timings and missing strata. Fewer than 10/12 complete
root triplets at either depth leaves cost acceptance UNKNOWN; no automatic rerun.

For a scoped engineering GO, require at least 5% reduction of summed
constructor-plus-outer-choose wall on eligible complete work, at least 8/12 root
medians faster, no eligible root median slower by over 10%, and no CPU aggregate
regression. These are prospective practical thresholds, not statistical confidence
or strength claims. State root coverage separately from repetition count and
retain all root ratios. Concurrent load, allocator state, Native memory and
peak total RSS remain limitations unless actually observed. A NO-GO stops this
candidate; an UNKNOWN remains UNKNOWN. Even a GO is only a restricted exact-cost
result: no default activation, new quality games or class-wide success follows.

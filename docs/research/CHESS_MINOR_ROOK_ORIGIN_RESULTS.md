## Rook limitation, and a sufficient stable relation

Castling's rook predicate uses baseR AND promoted=no. Native R and promoted P/R
at the relevant corner with the same enabled right give different legal choices.
Current geometry alone is therefore insufficient for a global imported-state
rook equivalence. The second castle movement checks base OR currentR, but the
earlier state guard already excludes the promoted rook; do not infer permission
from the move effect's weaker check alone.

A sufficient relation is: both own castling rights of the RELABELLED rook's
owner are explicitly0, other current board/aux/history correspond, and the
same F24F history bounds hold. These rights cannot reactivate: production rules
only clear rights/triggers, never set_bool1. R quiet/capture moves and all other
guards then fail to distinguish the origins; the relation persists. Rights of
the other owner need not be erased. Excluding only a currently available castle
is too weak: an imported empty corner with an enabled right could later receive
the tagged rook and expose the origin guard.

In an actually reachable game from the initial array, an enabled corner right
implies its initial native rook never left/was removed; those events permanently
clear that right. A promoted Pawn-origin replacement cannot arrive without such
an event. This is a rule-source reachability implication, not a validator added
to imported synthetic histories. Retain separate historical qualification.

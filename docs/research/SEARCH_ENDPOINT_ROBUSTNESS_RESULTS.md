# Endpoint agreement does not certify searched-mixture agreement

The frozen exposed algebra example establishes a limitation relevant to the
two declared price laws. Under both admitted integer coefficient endpoints,
root B uniquely wins. At their exact arithmetic midpoint, root A uniquely
wins.45 feature/point terms, no game transitions, labels or source queries.

Let w(t)=(1-t)w_geom+t*w_linear,0<=t<=1. The two A leaves score
z(t)=-17576+34494t and its negative, so A(t)=-abs(z(t)).
B(t)=-10283+46t. Solving A>B gives exactly
7293/34448 < t < 27859/34540. At those two boundaries A and B tie;
outside B uniquely wins. The A defender changes its active leaf at
t=17576/34494, inside the interval. This is an inference from the frozen
scores, not another root sample or parameter search.

Static action differences are linear, so testing all vertices of a convex
coefficient family suffices for a static all-parameter dominance certificate.
After a defender minimum, each action can instead be concave piecewise-linear;
the difference between two searched action values need not have its extreme
at the family endpoints. Therefore agreement of geometric/linear controllers
alone is not proof of robustness across their mixtures. The former exact
duration hull's static statements remain unchanged.

A valid one-dimensional searched certificate partitions the coefficient line
at all pairwise LEAF equality locations, not just model endpoints. On every
open interval the order of every leaf form is fixed, so all min/max nodes and
root choices are fixed; each boundary is evaluated separately for full ties.
For higher-dimensional families this becomes a cell-feasibility problem.
Do not replace joint cells with independent coefficient-box endpoints or
ignore terminal/unknown branches. No broad LP solver or production machinery
was built for this observation.

Mainline impact: keep the two frozen laws as two assumptions. A future complete
root trial may report both independently, or explicitly qualify a shared-family
robust statement. It must not infer the latter from endpoint agreement after
search. Neither price sensitivity nor robustness establishes better decisions
against an independent outcome reference.

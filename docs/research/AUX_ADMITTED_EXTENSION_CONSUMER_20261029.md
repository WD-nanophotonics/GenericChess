## Genuine existential errors without individual action labels

The same complete outcomes support a finite algebraic statement even though
this work creates no localized action-WDL certificate. Let V(h) be minimax seat0
utility for the literal finite H96/repetition4 game, with terminal W/L/D
utilities +1/-1/0. At a seat0 action, r0=V(h)-V(child); at a seat1 action,
r1=V(child)-V(h). These are nonnegative and each belongs to {0,1,2}.
For any complete played game, telescoping gives `z=V(root)-R0+R1`.

For a full-history-matched pair, write A for ON in seat0 and B for ON in seat1.
The same unknown starting V cancels:

`delta = zA-zB = R_OFF-R_ON = 2*(ON pair points-1)`.

| Pair | delta | Necessary ON loss | Necessary OFF loss |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| na-f0-d5-h0 | -1 | >=1 | >=0 |
| na-f0-d5-h1 | +1 | >=0 | >=1 |
| na-f3-d5-h1 | -2 | >=2 | >=0 |
| na-f6-d9-h0 | -2 | >=2 | >=0 |
| Other 11 pairs | 0 | >=0 | >=0 |

Summing per-pair inequalities gives ON experienced loss >=5 WDL units and OFF
>=1, with OFF-minus-ON total exactly -4. Since an individual strict action loss
is at most2, there must be at least three strict ON decisions and one strict OFF
decision somewhere in these disjoint trajectories. This is stronger than merely
using the aggregate difference to infer ON>=4, because positive and negative
pairs cannot cancel the per-pair nonnegativity constraints.

This is a reexpression of these same outcomes, **not independent evidence or a
new strength trial**. It identifies no mistaken action, best move or starting
value. A first divergence need not be the error; later mistakes can cancel, even
in draws. It does not certify an extension defect, causal root repair, H6/H128
truth or class-wide superiority. It does show why zero newly located errors
cannot be read as perfect play. These exposed outcome-selected pairs are not
new experiential training starts or a holdout.

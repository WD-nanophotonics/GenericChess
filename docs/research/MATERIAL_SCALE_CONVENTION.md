# Material scale is a declared gauge; relative values remain scientific claims

**Unknown.** Does the lack of a rule-given cardinal *unit* by itself prevent
testing any cheap static material-prior hypothesis?

**Smallest direct observation.** For a pure material evaluator
`E_w(s)=sum_t w_t n_t(s)`, every positive rescaling `E_{c w}=c E_w`
(`c>0`) preserves leaf ordering and pure material minimax choices, including
ties, at a fixed search contract. The two-action witness in
`ROOT_DECISION_IDENTIFIABILITY.md` also shows that different *ratios* can
give the same correct root decision. Thus a unit convention removes only a
gauge freedom; it does not identify the scientifically relevant ratios.

For any proposed raw nonnegative vector `w` with at least one positive
ordinary type, declare the normalized report `v_t=w_t/max_u w_u` over
ordinary types in the same RuleSet. This sets the largest predicted ordinary
type to 1. It is label invariant, unchanged by adding an exact duplicate
type with the same value, and inexpensive. It is a **reporting convention**,
not a rule-derived valuation theorem or a human-reference fit. The raw
construction must be frozen independently; normalization cannot repair a
wrong ordering or ratio. An all-zero vector or any nonfinite component fails
the convention instead of being silently assigned a unit.

This convention is per RuleSet. A production evaluator that adds mobility,
mate, or other fixed numerical terms still needs an explicit embedding scale
for material against those terms; common scaling of material alone can
change its choices. That deployment scale is separate from the structural
prior and must not be called rule-implied. The convention likewise does not
select which states to compare, how to weight them, or which prediction loss
to minimize.

**Decision.** Stop treating discovery of a unique absolute material unit as
a prerequisite to proposing a falsifiable approximation. Require a
predeclared gauge, then focus research on the relative vector and its
context-selection and validation objective. Do not retrofit this gauge to
rescue the failed V2/V2E candidates, and keep Xiangqi material values sealed.

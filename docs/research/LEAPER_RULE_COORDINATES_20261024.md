## Static mating capacity: an explicit scoped criterion

For any finite nonzero fixed Leap attack set O on a square n>=3, let

`O_active = {d in O : abs(dx)<n and abs(dy)<n}`.

Under the stated K+A versus bare-K local semantics, a static checkmate configuration exists **iff O_active contains two offsets at Manhattan distance1**. This concerns all local configurations of that roster, not reachability from a particular initial board or history.

Negative proof: if V is checked, every orthogonal neighbour is outside A's attack set when offsets are orthogonally independent. Nonadjacent Kings differ by at least2 in one coordinate; an available step perpendicular to that coordinate preserves royal separation. Capturing A removes its attack source. At least one legal escape remains.

Constructive direction: select an orthogonally adjacent pair and the offset with larger absolute value in its changing axis. Put V at the matching corner and A at V minus that offset. Place K0 two squares inward in the perpendicular axis. The other offset attacks one orthogonal escape; K0 covers the other and the diagonal. A differs from K0. It cannot occupy the unprotected orthogonal escape, since that would require the other offset to be zero. All pieces fit the active bounds. Extra A attacks cannot restore bare-K escapes. Eighty asymmetric minimal pairs in [-3,3]^2 were checked with the actual Core on4x4; this is source-assisted validation, not an independent engine or exhaustive2^48 search. Independent mathematical review remains useful.

For the original five D4 orbits, the five minimal positive masks are **3,5,10,12,24**; maximal negative sets are **9,17,22**. Eleven masks have no static mate;20 have corner certificates. Five primitive3x3 witnesses embed in every larger square. Actual Core checks cover all20 at3/4/5. Mixed mask17 is negative, so parity alone is incomplete.

There are24 static-capacity boundary edges in the75-edge cube. The23 parity-boundary candidates include two false positives (1,17),(16,17) and miss (17,19),(17,21),(17,25). A linear threshold on the five orbit bits cannot classify the whole static target: v3+v12=v6+v9, while the first two labels are positive and the latter two negative. The five pair interactions give an exact Boolean formula. This is not a generic price or NNUE impossibility claim.

A prospective longer-orbit check keeps the old generator unchanged: orth2 alone and orth3 alone are negative; their union admits a shared real A(0,1)->(0,3) action that changes stalemate to checkmate on4x4. On3x3, orth3 is inactive and the union is negative. Declared offsets must therefore be clipped before applying the criterion. Rays, conditional move/capture asymmetry, extra actors, promotion/drop reachability, auxiliary effects and different scoring are outside this iff claim.

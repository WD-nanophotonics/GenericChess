These are statements about the shallow material critic, not strategic piece
values. At the third root, the zero chosen-PV gradient sits at a nondifferentiable
point of |B−C|. At the fourth, the critic is locally constant while its optimal
action set changes. A numerical gradient, a local derivative and a policy
preference are different objects. This is not a library-bug finding or proof
that an entire TDLeaf episode is stuck: earlier eligibility and other states
can still update the parameters.

The local result is more than an interpolation through nine points. ABC leaf
coefficients lie in {-1,0,1}³. A leaf-order equality not passing through (1,1)
has an integer nonzero offset there; a displacement of infinity-norm less than
1/4 cannot cross it. The through-unit equalities form 12 rays. We check the
origin, each ray and two independent interior directions in each sector at
radius1/8: 37 exact rational prices. On each sector, all leaf orderings remain
fixed, so finite min/max returns one affine form. Continuity at the origin
and the two independent samples determine it; the saved forms and boundary
values establish the displayed local expressions. Floating-point deployment
at arbitrarily tiny perturbations is a separate, still unmeasured question.

Two producer mistakes were caught before their first search call: the capture
driver asserted the wrong channel-name shape, and the short-quality driver
matched action_to_dict against a different serializer. Their original sources,
failure logs and zero-call outcomes remain ignored. Repairs repeat no completed

## What the price-cell argument does and does not say

With fixed A=1, AB leaves have a+bB, with a,b in {-1,0,1}. Possible equality
cuts are -2,-1,-1/2,0,1/2,1,2: 15 open-cell/boundary representatives cover that
leaf-order arrangement in real arithmetic, inside a terminal-dominating safety
range. AAB still has one free relative price, but a can range from -2 to 2;
its sufficient arrangement has 13 cuts and 27 representatives. Some cuts need
not be reachable. These are not floating-point or resource-cut equivalences.

ABC can have two relative parameters, but stock, geometry and actual PVs can
reduce their exposure. Two free parameters are a useful design suggestion,
not a necessary admission gate for meaningful learning. More actors, numeric
gradients, a price grid and true action-WDL improvement remain separate claims.

Claude's latest comment is adopted with that qualification. The earlier +4
aggregate WDL was the two cheap priors versus uniform on a finite labelled
cohort, not an exhaustive best-constant upper envelope over all histories and
future trajectories. The previous TD pilot is closed: unit TD matched the free
prior and prior TD added nothing there. No further KAB training is launched.

# Finite owned-resource lifetime: next construction hypothesis

2026-10-04. Prospective independent approximation, no coefficients, new roots
or labels sampled. This is a different premise from static one-window net-token
transfer and unexplained Perron feedback. It asks about a token's future own
service while retaining ownership, with physical promotion/hand modes explicit.

## Defined surrogate

Track one ordinary physical token and a fixed owner. A mode is board/current
type or hand/base type. Anchors are excluded. One cycle is an actual own action
followed, unless terminal, by an actual opponent action. Use a frozen context
law and complete-choice policy, not an intrinsic opportunity existence as a
probability that a capture occurs. All actions/claims retain authoritative
effects/history. If some other own token acts, the tracked token can remain
idle; it is still exposed to the reply. Ownership loss is absorbing even when
the opponent receives that base token in hand. Promotion/drop change mode while
retaining own identity. A terminal/RESTART or unsupported mapping needs explicit
handling before estimates; never silently continue from a terminal child.

Give reward1 for each enemy ordinary physical token actually removed by the
tracked token's own action in that cycle,0 otherwise. This is unit removal
service, not human price or enemy resource value. A terminal goal has no extra
guessed material reward. Let g_m be expected reward from a cycle starting in
mode m, P_mn its probability of surviving in the same owner's mode n afterwards.
P is substochastic; the missing row mass is ownership loss or declared endpoint.
The future context is refreshed conditional on surviving mode n. That refresh
is the central approximate closure assumption, not a claim about real play.

For a predeclared finite H cycles, v^(0)=0 and
v^(h+1)=g+P v^(h). Equivalently v^(H)=sum_(k=0)^(H-1) P^k g.
Each term counts expected service in a distinct cycle of this surrogate; the
same removed enemy is not recursively credited as an executing attacker. No
discount coefficient or requirement to solve an eigenvector is introduced.
H must come from a declared time window, not fit validation labels. H=1 is the
old kind of one-window unit service and must not be presented as a new discovery.
H=2 is the smallest changed temporal question; larger H is not admitted by this
proposal and would magnify context-closure error without evidence.

For a one-mode illustration with capture service probability c and survival
probability s, H=2 gives c(1+s). If two tokens share c but differ in s, this
surrogate distinguishes them. If survival is common across modes and P does
not change relative future reward, it collapses to a common rescaling of g.
These are algebraic examples, not observed Chess/Shogi piece prices. Joint
same-context g/P cannot be replaced with independent sparse marginals without
an additional assumption; the observed motif covariance warns against that.

Unlike an infinite fundamental-matrix claim, finite H remains defined for
nonabsorbing classes. Infinite sum could diverge or need a transience proof;
we do not select a damping constant to force it finite. v is nonnegative, but
some reachable modes may have zero rewards at H=2. Positive static material
coverage is therefore a gate, not a guessed epsilon or a borrowed hand premium.

## Minimum falsifier before any empirical batch

First inspect whether public actions permit exact tracking through promotion,
drop and enemy capture-to-hand in both owner directions, including multiple
same-type tokens. Existing Piece owner/base fields do not alone prove identity.
Next specify the conditional context/action law and check it is not the spent
all-quiet service corpus: g=0 everywhere would again yield v=0 for every H.
Do not sample just to rediscover that algebraic failure. No labels are needed
to state this gate, but an independently selected law still needs motivation.

An exact joint two-cycle calculation on the SAME bounded contexts can compare
true mode-conditioned continuation to g+Pg. Replacement threats, shared blockers,
ownership conversion and opponent action selection can invalidate closure.
Freeze identities, contexts, actions, caps and the decision-changing tolerance
before observation; do not retune P/H on exposed goal or human labels.
Existing per-experiment caps remain in force; a larger two-cycle budget must not
be smuggled in as many small batches. Required held/promoted support is not yet
complete and Xiangqi historical/human holdout remains untouched.

## Context/action feasibility gates after trace qualification

OWNED_TAG_TRACE_RESULTS.md now qualifies ten small public identity controls.
This removes a concrete implementation doubt, not the context law. For each
owned mode m, future work must specify mu_m over COMPLETE states and a declared
tag law. Keep board base/current and held base support, side orientation,
absolute ply/history and terminal/claim handling. A board/current aggregate may
mix several base origins (for example a native Q and promoted P); that origin
mixture cannot be silently chosen by the estimator. State it, or refine modes.

Whole legal choices, uniformly sampled if that is the chosen policy, must first
be completely enumerated. Uniform destinations/captures would be a different
policy and can hide promotion/semantic branches. Unsupported/RESTART endpoints
are not ordinary P rows; freezing a proposal requires deciding whether they
are censored or legitimate stop endpoints under the declared service objective.
This decision is independent of observed coefficients or useful goal labels.

An exact complete H=2 tree has four action plies. Even128 choices/state can
require128^4 paths, incompatible with current small caps. Exact first-cycle
own/reply enumeration can already require128^2 child paths. Do not advertise
a cheap complete matrix from a smaller unexplained subset. A predeclared sampled
path law can preserve complete local choices with four real transitions per
path, but yields estimates/uncertainty and mode coverage, not an exact kernel.
Reserve all seeds and per-game totals before measuring; do not run successive
small batches until a preferred ordering emerges or a zero entry turns positive.

Same-context closure is a DIFFERENT target from reproducing the surrogate.
On an independently reserved start population/path sample, freeze reward/mode
estimates first, compare actual second-cycle tagged service with the predicted
continuation for the first child's surviving mode, and report the paired residual
and uncertainty. A root death contributes zero future own service. Refreshing
ordinary victims or resetting child history is part of the approximation, not
allowed in the actual continuation. Mode averaging can hide blockers, geometry,
victim depletion, side/count changes and held base origin. The synthetic1 versus
1.5 control demonstrates why a mode marginal alone does not validate closure.

A full initial-inventory law may be too quiet to estimate reward under the cap;
a sparse law may not represent full-inventory play. In Shogi, missing physical
tokens on both board/hands must be explicitly synthetic, not reachable standard
inventory. A single-enemy ordinary pool disappears after one capture, making
refreshed repeated service a replenishment model; do not run it as a faithful
two-cycle lifetime test. Choose at least a genuinely new feasible context law,
with its purpose/coverage limitation, before numbers. These gates permit an
approximate construction; they do not require a unique rule-given population.

No estimator/pilot is frozen or executed by this section. The immediate task is
to settle a resource-preserving, mode-complete law and a bounded falsifier whose
result can change the construction decision. A statistical service vector must
later face independently frozen leaf-choice use evidence, not only its own
closure statistic, and remain separate from Xiangqi human holdout.

## Targeted H=2 closure residual

Under a fixed declared complete-choice policy, let r(s) be expected tracked
unit reward for one cycle from full state/tag s, and K(s,dz) its substochastic
full-state continuation law. It stops on ownership loss or a declared endpoint.
For each mode m take reference context law mu_m, g_m=E_mu_m r(s), and
P_mn=E_mu_m K(s,mode n). Let nu_mn be the actual next-context law conditional
on start mode m and surviving next mode n; terms with P_mn=0 are omitted.
The true two-cycle mean from mu_m is
g_m + sum_n P_mn E_nu_mn r(z), whereas the refreshed surrogate is
g_m + sum_n P_mn g_n. Their signed difference is therefore
e_m=sum_n P_mn(E_nu_mn r(z)-E_mu_n r(z)).

This derivation is our finite fixed-policy inference, not a claim that board
types form an exact Markov decision process. It isolates the new decision-
changing check: induced child reward mean versus reference mode reward mean,
under the same freeze and WITHOUT resetting the actual child. Full transition
equivalence is stronger than needed for this H=2 population mean. A zero mean
residual can hide individual errors and cannot validate H>2 or another policy.
If each conditional mean difference is bounded by epsilon_mn, then
|e_m|<=sum_n P_mn epsilon_mn. Present Chess/Shogi single-victim unit rewards
lie in[0,1], so an unqualified conditional difference can be as large as1.
That worst-case bound is honest but not evidence of a useful approximation.

Independent exact synthetic controls in test_finite_owned_service.py verify
this decomposition: two modes with different context mixtures give X error3/4
and Y error9/32, matching full two-cycle branch enumeration. They exercise the
closure statistic itself, not a new game population, coefficient fit or label.

Physical tag survival and continuation eligibility differ. If an own action
ends the game, its surviving physical tag may still have mass1, while future
service has continuation mass0. A terminal own capture can have current reward1
and no future cycle. Feeding raw tag survival alone into P would incorrectly
predict another reward. RESTART must obey a predeclared service endpoint or
censoring policy; its unresolved goal is never converted into a draw label.

[Li, Walsh and Littman2006](https://thomasjwalsh.net/pub/aima06Towards.pdf),
section3 definitions1/3, explicitly separates weighted abstract rewards/
transitions from the stronger model-irrelevance condition preserving one-step
reward and transition mass into each abstract class. Our inference: merely
writing a well-formed averaged P does not establish fidelity. Their discounted
MDP planning/learning guarantees are not automatically our finite fixed-policy
service or Chess WDL guarantees. No learned model, discount or worker is added.

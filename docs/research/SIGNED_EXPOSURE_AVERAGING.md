# From signed exposure to a type approximation: prospective construction

2026-10-04. New premise: use actual action consequences and a declared context
average, rather than multiplying sparse incidence means or interpreting every
removed edge as one protected token. This is an executable construction proposal,
not an admitted material vector or a new empirical corpus.

## Positional quantity and units

For each selected surviving own ordinary entity k, let T(s,k) be existence of
an intrinsic enemy board-removal opportunity with the COMPLETE board occupancy.
This counterfactual enemy window ignores dynamic attacker anchor safety and
history, and is not a legal null move. For an actual public legal own action a,
track that same entity to its new square/current type in child tau(s,a), and
record d_k=T(s,k)-T(tau(s,a),k) in{-1,0,1}. Keep attacker sources as well as the
boolean: several removed/new edges can leave T unchanged. Anchors are excluded.

The actor is either a separately reported entity or explicitly part of the
selected survivor set; do not randomly alias it with the third motif token.
Promotion changes its current type, not its identity. Unsupported custody/hand
mapping is incomplete, not a zero-exposure held bonus. Missing identities or
unqualified intrinsic semantics fail closed. This permits replacement threats
and newly opened lines, unlike the old unsigned removed-edge motif.

Sum d_k is a change in the number of exposed selected entities. It is NOT the
number the opponent can remove simultaneously, avoided loss or a WDL estimate.
A one-capture action window can expose many alternatives. No human prices or
arbitrary positive clipping are included. Same-root children tau(s,a),tau(s,b)
can be compared directly without making the parent enemy window a legal turn.

## One explicit averaging hypothesis

Define a context population mu BEFORE any coefficients or goal labels. A small
future pilot could use fresh actual-inventory ongoing Q contexts and both owners
under its declared structural support, subject to existing proposal/transition
caps. Current sparse two/three-token activity populations are not that law.
Choose an ordinary physical actor uniformly among its owner's tokens; average
over its complete canonical public actions uniformly, including distinct
promotion/semantic branches. A token without an available action contributes
the declared zero *opportunity*, not evidence that its intrinsic worth is zero.
Record action availability separately. No goal/candidate performance filter.

Let r(s,i) be that action mean of summed signed exposure changes for a fixed
declared survivor selection. A proposed type statistic is E_mu[r(s,i)|current
type i=t], with owner directions kept separate before a verified symmetry merge.
It measures conditional protective action capacity under this policy/population,
not a theorem about possession value. Mixing a per-available-action average with
an unconditional mean would change the premise: availability cannot disappear
in normalization. Uniform canonical actions can depend on the rule encoding;
duplicating a semantically equivalent pattern is a representation-sensitivity
falsifier. Do not silently quotient actions by destination or physical capture
when successor history or promotion modes differ.

This construction may produce zero or negative entries. Taking absolute values,
adding a common constant, clipping or blending with offense would introduce a
new hypothesis and could change material-sensitive choices. It is not an
automatic positive-vector candidate for MATERIAL_LEAF_DECISION_DESIGN.md.
Promotion/held modes and actual deployment need separate coverage; a board-only
current-type statistic does not meet the cross-game objective by itself.

## Why fixed-inventory regression is not an alternative shortcut

In a context law with fixed unpromoted board counts and no hands, static material
count features are constant. Their centered covariance is zero: positional
exposure variation alone cannot identify relative linear material coefficients.
Projection onto that constant inventory would identify at most an intercept.
Changing inventory, considering child count differences or declared physical
interventions can provide variation, but defines a new law; choose it before
measurements. This does not forbid approximate construction. It prevents calling
an unidentifiable fit a uniquely rule-derived type vector.

## Smallest useful next checks

First qualify entity tracking and signed effects on same-root public choices:
an action removing an enemy can replace its threat (already verified), while
a quiet action can expose a previously sheltered selected token. Show the
actor's separate exposure to reveal whether aggregate unit-risk merely moved
between entities. These are semantic controls, not ranking/use evidence.

Then decide if conditional protective capacity is worth a bounded statistic at
all: current objective wants cheap static material, while this quantity requires
actions, legal child effects and a strong context/action law. If retained,
freeze the population, survivor policy, availability handling, exact action
identity, mode coverage and original caps before observing any coefficients.
If not retained, switch to an independently motivated finite resource-lifetime
or exchange approximation; do not add a guessed positive offset or rerun old
global net-token service to rescue this proposal.

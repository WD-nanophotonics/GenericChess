# Scaling rule-derived promotion gain — 2026-10-21

## Question and fixed contrast

After the bonus-on/off interaction panel found no resolved reward or first-action gain, replay of its exact saved trajectories showed that the bonus often changed candidate-side static scores. On the six positive gains observed there (302, 331, 734, 774, 1013, 1159), the existing `max(1, gain // 1000)` mapping assigns the same integer unit. This fresh, registered 5x5 panel asked whether retaining more gain magnitude changes finite-budget utility. It compared the current integer-unit mapping against zone bonus `(3 * gain + 500) // 1000`, with one-step bonus half the zone value. Leaf prices, all other evaluator terms, rules, foe evaluator, search options, and limits were held fixed. The foe had the bonus disabled in both arms. Eight new generated-rule seeds (622107–622114), 16 legal-random roots, both candidate owners, 48 candidate plies, and D3/8192-node/0.5-second/q0, TT-off search yielded 32 paired root-owner contrasts. No product code/default changed.

## Results

All 64 registered slots completed in 93.1827 seconds: 64 actual terminals, zero UNKNOWN, invalid actions, replay mismatches, or unstarted slots. Current and gain-scaled arms each recorded 15 wins, 15 losses, and 2 draws. The UNKNOWN-aware paired reward-difference bounds were exactly `[0,0]` overall, by owner, and by root parity. At 198 candidate decision states with identical full-history hashes, the selected actions matched in every case; first candidate actions also matched across all paired roots.

The current arm searched 156,349 candidate nodes versus 156,201 for gain-scaled (148 fewer; 0.095% aggregate reduction); median paired node reduction was 0. Candidate search elapsed sums were 22.190 s versus 22.314 s (+0.56%); total caller sums were 43.988 s versus 44.185 s (+0.45%). These short local elapsed totals are descriptive and not a controlled machine-wall benchmark. Under the preregistered joint rule, the reward gate (strictly positive conservative lower bounds overall and for each owner) failed, while the cost guard passed (no more than 10% median node increase). The candidate is therefore not retained because utility was not demonstrated, not because its cost guard failed; the panel is not widened.

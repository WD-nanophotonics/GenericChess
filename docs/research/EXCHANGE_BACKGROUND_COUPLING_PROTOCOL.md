# Frozen background-coupling control

Predeclared after diagnosing the fixed physical-placement pilot's zeros, before
executing this control. Question: does the net-custody task suppress a focal
capture solely because the opponent can capture a different token elsewhere?
This is an objective diagnosis, not another piece-value fitting or recapture probe.

Four synthetic roots, two Chess and two Standard Shogi. Keep owner-0 K (0,0),
focal R (3,3), opposing P (3,4), empty hands and side 0. Put owner-1 K at
(n-1,n-2) and owner-1 R at (n-2,n-1). The exposed-background context has an
owner-0 Pawn at (n-2,n-2); the shifted-background control moves ONLY that Pawn
to (n-3,n-2). Same inventory, same focal source, same target and turn, no Chess
castling/en-passant rights, reset synthetic history. Zero-based coordinates.

Before execution prediction: the focal legal-action sets are identical within
each game. The unpromoted R capture at (3,4) secures a positive two-ply net gain
in the shifted control; in the exposed context the opponent can capture the
background Pawn without capturing the focal R, cancelling the gain. This gives
best-focal-action task scores shifted=1/exposed=0. Full enumeration may falsify
this through an overlooked action, terminal win or different legal reply.

Apply the existing task unchanged. Exhaust all focal actions and replies, then
replay the recorded refutation for the selected capture and certify the focal
R remains on (3,4). Count board-plus-hand custody under its current owner.
Limits: four roots, 2,000 materialized transitions, 10 seconds and a 15-second
external timeout. No additional sampled population or more generous search cap.

A pass would establish sensitivity to background liability, not invalidate every
approximate context average. Whole-board gain can be an intentional objective;
calling it focal survivability would be incorrect. A nonzero population prior
and additive material use remain separate hypotheses. Do not replace the utility
after seeing this result merely to make a preferred vector. Record the issue for
the next substantive advisor discussion while independent work continues.

## Mainline decision

Adopt frozen action-choice regret as an available validation contract alongside
goal-prediction risk, never as a universal coefficient derivation. Avoid forcing
uncalibrated leaf scores into WDL units. Service-count failure still rejects
its specific tested use, not every possible search use; no failed vector is
revived. A new cheap independently motivated construction and independently
chosen material-sensitive deployment remain missing. Address those premises
rather than adding mate fixtures or fitted weights.

The restricted no-promotion cells match the previous producer exactly, and all
promotion contrasts are nonnegative. This is a declared partial mechanism
toggle, not complete Chess semantics or independently established pawn prices.
The data retain both normalizations over the same actor population. Neither
vector has been promoted or selected for agreement with human references.

Counterfactual baselines have related uses in credit assignment, including
[COMA](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/people/shimon.whiteson/pubs/foersteraaai18.pdf) and
[Difference Rewards Policy Gradients](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.11258v2).
Those works assume a task reward and response/learning setup; they do not
derive universal chess-piece prices. Our virtual ability task and interpretation
above are local constructions, not results asserted by those papers.

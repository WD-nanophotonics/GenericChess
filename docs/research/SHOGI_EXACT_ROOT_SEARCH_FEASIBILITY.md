# The fresh69-child root is not a naive full-depth2 opportunity

Before any reply enumeration, the saved NEW69-child root gives a strict lower
bound on full two-ply physical width:69 root events plus at least one enemy
reply per child is138, already above the unchanged128-event cap.

Every saved child keeps the opponent King at i9. With only opposing Kb1 and
P d5 plus at most one new Pawn drop, at least one of h8/h9 is safe and reachable
by the King. P d5 attacks d6 only. A new Pawn attacks just its next rank on the
same file, so cannot attack both h8 and h9; if it occupies one of them the King
may capture it, since neither Kb1 nor P d5 protects that cell. The quiet Pawn
move attacks d7 only. Occupancy of i8 and resulting check do not change this
two-square witness. Thus no child lacks all legal replies. This is a scoped
geometric lower bound, not enumeration or formal-game outcome evidence.

Do not begin a naive full-depth2 batch on this exposed root. A common global
coefficient dominance reduction might avoid physical width, but requires a
NEW justified bound before events; no budget increase, leaf subset or different
partial trees. Alternatively choose a prospective candidate-independent narrow
opportunity with independently qualified goal/source scope. The one-ply exact
drop gap remains a mechanism, not proof that dropping improves strategy.

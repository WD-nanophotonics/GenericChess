# Frozen common-context exchange pilot

Predeclared on 2026-10-03 before execution. This is an applicability and task
diagnostic, not a material-reference comparison or production coefficient.
Do not change the contexts/weights after seeing scores.

## Motivation and common law

The declared task is securing one ordinary-token custody gain through one
adversarial reply. A first population diagnostic should include both target
geometry and resistance; another selected capture alone would not test optimal
choice over all focal actions. Use an explicit small tactical environment law,
independent of reference values: equal mass on three fixed physical contexts.
This is a deliberately restricted law, not uniform all-board placement, actual
game visitation, or an assertion that the three motifs occur equally often.

In each game put owner-0 K at (0,0), the owner-0 focal board token at (3,3),
owner-1 K at (n-1,n-2), owner 0 to move, and empty hands. Coordinates are
zero-based file/rank. Chess has no castling or en-passant entitlement; histories
are reset synthetic histories. These contexts are not claimed initial-reachable.

| Common context | Opposing token placement | Mass |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| diagonal_open | Pawn at (4,4) | 1/3 |
| orthogonal_open | Pawn at (3,4) | 1/3 |
| diagonal_defended | Pawn at (4,4), Rook at (4,6) | 1/3 |

The varying inventory across contexts is explicitly part of this finite mixture.
Within any context, all focal types share the same environment inventory and
positions. The corner frame avoids the nonmoving king being initially checked
by the tested ray pieces. Own-anchor safety and nonmoving-anchor safety are
checked. Any invalid/unsupported context aborts scoring the common law; do not
discard it for one type and renormalize that type's remaining contexts.

Chess focal types: B,R,Q. Standard Shogi controls: B,R. These five long-range
types times three contexts give 15 roots. Selection tests the shared ray/combined-
ray contract, not all ordinary types. Pawn/Knight/promoted/held coverage and the
full placement ensemble remain open. Enemy Pawn and optional Rook are declared
motif fixtures, not fitted prices or a general game-independent inventory rule.

## Objective, enumeration and falsifiers

Enumerate every public legal first action at the focal source, including all
promotion branches, then every legal opponent reply. Win=success, terminal
draw/loss=failure, no-contest unsupported; other ongoing successors succeed iff
the acting owner's ordinary board-plus-hand custody balance increased. Empty
nonterminal reply sets are errors. No focal action means zero task success.
The root score is max-action/min-reply binary success. Average with the fixed
three common weights; retain raw rational scores without a production gauge.

No action or reply pruning based on success is permitted in this audit. Store
action evidence, reply counts and first refutations. Core streaming handles and
checkpoints bound enumeration; no parallel job or additional Agent is used.
Hard aborts: 16 roots, 20,000 materialized legal transitions, 30 seconds total.
External execution also has a 35-second subprocess timeout. An abort produces
no complete vector, never zero success or draw for truncated evidence.

Predictions from the declared geometry, before execution: B can secure the open
diagonal token, R the open orthogonal token, Q either open token. The defended
diagonal capture can be refuted by R recapture. Expected scores are Chess
B=1/3,R=1/3,Q=2/3 and Shogi B=1/3,R=1/3. These are task-contract predictions,
not a material-value hypothesis or human-reference gate. Exact full enumeration

If complete, compare action-set success and costs, not human piece values.
Even the predicted result would only show conditional differentiation under
this declared law. It cannot establish the common law, two-ply utility,

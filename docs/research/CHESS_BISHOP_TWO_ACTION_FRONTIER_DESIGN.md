For a same-colour nonaligned displacement(dx,dy), put a=(dx+dy)/2 and
b=(dx-dy)/2. Both are integers and nonzero. The only two-diagonal elbows are
q1=s+(a,a), q2=s+(b,-b). Every two-action contact must use one of them; opposite
colour is unreachable and blocked aligned contact cannot detour in2.

One valid elbow gives one route of |a|+|b|=max(|dx|,|dy|) actions-in-squares
ray length, with max(|dx|,|dy|)-1 blocker destinations on its union of legs
excluding s/d but INCLUDING the elbow once. Thus62 minus that count blocker
placements have exact contact distance2. Two valid elbows have disjoint routes
except s/d, so one blocker never disables both: all62 blocker worlds take2.
Zero valid elbows means no2 proof, not unreachable. Do not normalize that mass.

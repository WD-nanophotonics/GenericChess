  reduction. Fresh finite-budget results do not support a general speed/strength
  claim or a larger default search budget.

  change q defaults or treat completion/depth as decision usefulness.
- Keep the opening256 filter. Opening1024 permits a different distribution;
  its higher generation yield is not a same-task acceleration.
- Keep geometry caching private. Value-key normalization repairs an observed
  accepted-input failure, but the new small-input timings regress and only two
  existing inputs were checked. No unconditional compiler cache adoption.
- Measure actual discarded quiet-child history cost before changing search.
  Availability and restricted material-ordering views already omit unused keys;
  recursive search retains complete history. Endpoint savings are insufficient
  to justify an unconditional history shortcut.

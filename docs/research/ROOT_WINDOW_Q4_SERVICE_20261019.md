## Actual consumer boundary

The replay retains 1046 ordinary PV-leaf / root-score differences. The existing
API promises a legal continuation, not equality with its literal leaf. Core and
Native scalar agreement above does not supply a missing q principal-leaf path.

A separate [after-seal consumer supplement](data/q4_leaf_consumer_20261019/README.md)
legally replays all 1046 stored leaf records / 40,896 commits. Raw leaf
quantization differs by at most .0269375 in human units, while substituting the
root score for the actual leaf predictor changes tanh outputs by a median
absolute .076624; 442 exceed .1. Mate-valued root scores versus nonterminal
ordinary PV leaves account for some extreme gaps. This is a hypothetical
substitution diagnostic, not an observed learning error or gain.

All 39 gap cases returning ordinary q0 have actual PV leaves in check and
hard-check depth 8. They directly illustrate why ordinary-q0 in a q4 request
must not be relabelled pure static q0.

Current product collect_self_play and Arena explicitly request q0. Collection
and tdleaf_update recompute the frozen predictor from actual leaf features;
they do not substitute result.score. No current consumer bug is established,
no collector/trainer is run, and no AUX learner or its configuration is changed.
Native q4 service evidence does not automatically authorize a credit migration.

[TreeStrap's original paper, sections 3–4](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2009/file/389bc7bb1e1c2a5e7e147703232a88f6-Paper.pdf)
distinguishes fixed search targets from predictor gradients and uses one-sided
losses for alpha-beta bounds. Its update sums over contributing nodes. Our
inference: correct root choices or valid individual bounds do not establish
equivalent sample weights, history identity, or credit. The paper supplies
method context, not independent verification of this project.

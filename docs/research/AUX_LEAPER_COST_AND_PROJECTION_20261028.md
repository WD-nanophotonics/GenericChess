## Exact restricted projection

Fix a trusted compiled IR and an admitted typed Position. Admission uses the
existing strict plain-Leaper footprint certificate: no ray, active drop, pass,
promotion, path/state/slot/zone guard, auxiliary effect, trigger or adjudication
extension. The finite witness additionally requires at most64 cells, no hand or
auxiliary contents, base=current with promoted false, matching fingerprint and
shape, integer owners, and exactly one anchor per owner. It does not widen the
certificate or claim admission of arbitrary newly constructed IR.

Let O[p,c] be the bitset of squares occupied by owner p/current type c. Let
R[p,c,t] be the static bitset of sources whose **capture geometry** reaches
target t. The attack-source set is

    S[p,t] = union_c (O[p,c] intersect R[p,c,t]).

There are no blockers or state guards in the admitted capture paths. The
executor's pseudoattack predicate checks capture geometry/guards and deliberately
omits own-anchor safety, postconditions and target occupancy. Consequently
`S[p,t] != empty` is precisely that pseudoattack predicate, including attacks on
empty or friendly-occupied targets and attacks by pinned actors. It is not an
executable capture or a legal-action set. Quiet geometry must be kept separate.

For a legal mover of owner p/type c from s to t:

    O'[p,c] = (O[p,c] without s) union {t}

and a captured opponent/type d is removed from O[1-p,d] at t. Other occupancy
groups stay unchanged. The corresponding source-set update clears s from every
S[p,x], inserts t exactly where c's capture geometry from t reaches x, and
clears a captured t from every opponent S[1-p,x]. Untouched sources remain.
Saved old values of touched groups, restored in reverse edit order, invert the
operation, including two edits to the mover's same group. An exception before
commit must restore each completed edit prefix; the offline witness verifies
this algebra, not a hypothetical integration into Core's transaction.

Importantly, XOR deletion from an attacked-target **union** is wrong when two
sources overlap. In the published n5-f0-h2 root, owner0 sources0,2,10 attack
target5. Clearing source0 must retain sources2 and10. The finite witness finds
this on actual retained data. The old Boolean implementation already preserves
overlap with prefix/suffix unions; this is an invariant for future work, not a
newly discovered product bug.

## Decision and next boundary

The theorem and finite witness support the **restricted geometric projection**,
not a production implementation. Occupancy alone omits side/rule binding,
absolute clock, history and public pattern/geometry action identities. The
base/current/promoted fields are recoverable only because admission fixes their
relationship. Endpoint deduplication is valid for attack existence, while
collapsing distinct public actions can invalidate enumeration/order/history.

Core's selected transition, checked gives-check, exact occurrence insertion,
snapshot/history context, terminal precedence and exception rollback still need
their original authority. In particular, a failed snapshot after occurrence
insertion but before history append is not repaired merely by restoring board
bits. Frame, occurrence and imported-key bridge rollback remain part of a future
implementation's obligation. No such implementation is made here.

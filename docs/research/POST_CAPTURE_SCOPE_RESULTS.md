# Post-capture labels preserve check, opponent hands and history

The separately prehashed POST_CAPTURE_VALIDATION_CONTRACT.md declares the
board-capability target, victim-type-stratified legal-capture deployment law,
two reference-only baselines and ten-percent squared-error margin before any
candidate coefficients or validation corpus labels. The margin is an engineering
choice, not a rule-selected quantity. The two existing admitted boards below
are only its first applicability/cost fixtures, not that independent corpus.

Each synthetic parent is reset to owner 1 to move with its quiet physical board
unchanged. Complete legal-action enumeration finds the following ordinary
owner-0 victim capture coverage. Terminal successors are recorded but excluded
from the declared ongoing deployment; no partial stratum is renormalized.

| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Chess | 49 | 12 | B, N, P, Q, R |
| Standard Shogi | 29 | 9 | B, P, R, S |

Chess therefore has a legal ongoing capture witness for every initial ordinary
victim type on this one parent. Shogi lacks G/L/N in this fixture's capture set;
that does not prove missing population support. Future equal-type deployment

The deterministic selection required by the frozen scope gate gives Chess
Q(3,6)xB(3,4), whose actual child is ongoing and checks owner 0. Shogi's
B(5,0)xP(4,1) promotes to TB; its ongoing actual child gives owner 1 a held Pawn.
Owner 0 has no hand token in either child. Existing own ordinary BOARD actor
counts fall from 15 to 14 Chess and from 19 to 18 Shogi, with B and P count
respectively reduced by one. The opponent's move, promotion, hand and check
changes are retained: this is not context-preserving deletion.

All remaining actors' complete first actions and replies were enumerated on
the ACTUAL child GameState. No quiet-root, empty-opponent-hand or initial-count
filter is imposed. Chess has two ordinary-actor evasion actions in this checked
root; Shogi has 53 ordinary-actor first actions, with 50 legal opponent Pawn-drop
replies across their branches. A drop recurring in different branches counts
as a reply in each branch; 50 is not a count of globally distinct destinations.
Both scope fixtures have capability count zero. Those labels do not measure
population means, coefficient quality or the frozen validation risk criterion.

The audit uses 1,988 materializations / 0.766 seconds, including compilation,
within the predeclared 10,000/15-second gate. Raw input/program/protocol hashes,
parent capture coverage, selected captures, full child board/hands/auxiliary/
history/repetition/ply evidence, actor actions and first refutations are in
data/post_capture_scope_20261004.json. No Core or production evaluator changes.

Five focused tests independently apply selected captures through public
apply_action and compare complete state evidence, verify public first-action
coverage and legal evasions, enumerate public replies including hand drops,
replay refutations, check exact cost/evidence reconstruction, and reject aborts
or unsupported own-hand actors. The earlier strict initial-inventory audit is
unchanged and is not silently broadened into a deployment validator.

## Decision

Adopt the frozen narrow validation design and this scope implementation. Legal
capture supplies real inventory variation while keeping causally changed state;
checked roots and opponent hands can be labelled at low bounded cost on the
two fixtures. The sampling law is a balanced sensitivity diagnostic, not a
claim of representative human/strategic play. Board-actor capability remains
a surrogate target, never material utility, custody prediction or terminal
decision regret. A passing finite diagnostic would still need separate material
validation and promoted/own-hand scope before a general prior claim.

Next freeze finite reference and independent deployment corpus generation,
seeds, counts, complete victim-stratum coverage and resource gates BEFORE type
estimates. All-zero reference signal, zero baseline risk, missing strata or
incomplete enumeration cannot qualify a useful nontrivial prior; do not tune
coefficients/margins or expand a failed budget. Keep reference/deployment data
separate and do not reuse the two scope fixtures as held-out validation. A
sampling/cost pass alone is not permission to skip that freeze. Xiangqi human
values remain sealed and the scientific objective remains open.

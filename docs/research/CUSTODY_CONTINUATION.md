## Executable approximation and state boundary

Let s retain full semantic state/history, and z the tag's square or hand owner.
Both players choose uniformly among canonical legal actions. For a same-base
hand of multiplicity k, a matching drop selects this latent tag with probability
1/k. Exchangeable provenance is explicit: aggregate hands do not identify
individual captured pieces. This law is chosen, not forced by the rules.

Reward r(s,z,a) is+1 for an ordinary capture made by the tag under original
owner0, or-1 under owner1. Capturing the tag transfers custody and resets current
type to the base through Core semantics, without a circular price penalty.
Repeated captures are service events, not permanent entity destruction. Anchor
captures carry no ordinary-service reward. The finite task has u_0=0 and
u_h(s,z)=mean over legal a of [r(s,z,a)+E_z' u_(h-1)(T(s,a),z')].
Terminal states stop without extra WDL payoff. Continuation remains in full
states; no common discounted hand coefficient replaces them.

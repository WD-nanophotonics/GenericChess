## Primary literature and inference

[Liu et al.2017](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1703.06275) use relative agent performance
as approximate skill-depth fitness and study parameter optimization with costly,
noisy playtests. This motivates testing a particular budget proxy's transfer;
it does not establish a calibrated strength chain for these deterministic
AlphaBeta policies or make seed repeats independent.

[Jaffe et al.2012](https://homes.cs.washington.edu/~zoran/jaffe2012ecg.pdf) formulate
balance through restricted play against a restriction-exploiting reference;
their prototype uses optimal agents. Our finiteD3 reference has no such oracle
claim. A near-zero reference mean is not strategic balance or human game quality.

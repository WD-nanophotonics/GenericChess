## B2 — independent direction, opportunity cost, counterfactual (formed from the user's goal)

The target is transferable **search quality at matched inclusive service**, ending in actual playable strength on distinct fully executable, precisely typed pure-Leaper games. The immediate question is whether a **generically stated decision/resource-allocation mechanism** can turn a stable complete-history search decision into a *better* decision on new families. A bounded AND/OR forced-threat or proof/disproof subsearch with **LOSS/DRAW/UNKNOWN** kept separate, or an explicitly context-conditioned continuation/history policy beyond current counters, is a candidate; both require a cheap source-level test for what existing Qsearch already sees. A second route is compiling a certified restricted rule domain (owner-relative LeapAtom sets, precomputed forward/reverse destination bitsets, occupancy and two-square reversible deltas) into one inclusive execution kernel, without changing full terminal/clock/history authority. Both are directions the user previously raised; mathematical Leaper compilation is not a new patrol invention.

The two latest meaningful research intervals are now concrete. Earlier PRIMARY produced bounded static integer-mask cost signals (8.06% root-entry / 6.79% charged-cold wall, with an unresolved earlier CPU-zero eligibility gate) and the first threat preflight ended without its planned 64/32 calls. Since the 08:02 JST patrol, PRIMARY made two sandbox commits (025c70cb HEAD, [compare](https://github.com/WD-nanophotonics/GenericChess/compare/48749ec334c9eb42955a844f06e1d101bb2a02e2...025c70cb4d35c8d2196b4a8d8dc0c9c1f2da75b8)): its 16 literal 8x8 families/layouts passed 16,180 two-ply Core transitions; it then paid for 64 threat-qualification, 64 incremental-royal, 64 Native binding, 128 factorial-hash and 192 resource-curve returns, **512 saved research Player returns in all and zero games**. This gives reusable legality/measurement and runtime knowledge: 13/16 eligible threat pairs across 8 families had post-budget changed-rank child entries, but **0 eligible families had stable OFF/ON root choice change**; the fixed 32 games were not started. Local-only hash reduced pooled matched full D3 call wall to 0.93485852 and CPU to 0.93421439 (14/16 complete groups). Binding and incremental-royal saved only ~1.33% and ~1.36%, below their 5% gates. Position-hash memo added no measured material benefit. An equal-service 192-return curve changed retained choices and depth at **1 second only in families 2 and 6**, not across most families. Changed depth D1/Q0→D2/Q4 confounds which search stage caused any difference; none is a value certificate. These inputs are development-exposed; neither 16 layouts nor 512 returns are independent family-level strength samples.

Over the same period AUX spent a reported 60.294989 effective minutes (3737.699354 confirmed seconds less 120-second reserve) on representation and independent endpoint consumption, with exactly two capped cold D1 root calls. Its 608-member byte-bound reader checked 64 qualification actions/PV, retained witnesses and 128 timing records, and independently replayed 192 curve returns/100 opponent replies through the shared Core. All 100 opponent replies remain ONGOING; full WDL is UNKNOWN. On a bijectively renamed game, both actual D1 selections stayed inside the **same 17-way optimal-within-D1 argmax**, yet the selected literal action differed because lexical tie-breaking depends on type labels. This is an instructive representation/selection control, **not** a strategic quality regression or new rule-family strength. AUX also shows that capture-to-inert-hand can preserve restricted reachable game histories while changing learner feature channels and gradients; typed repetition identities and cache lifetimes still need independent checks. These are bounded source/semantics results, not independently solved game trees.

**Counterfactual:** Continuing PRIMARY's proposed 32 matched-seat games for *only* the local-hash candidate would pay up to 32×(96−3)×1 s = **2,976 nominal decision-service seconds ≈49.6 minutes**, before replay/constructor/verification; its stated 32×125 s game ceilings total **66.7 minutes**, exceeding its separate 60-minute batch ceiling. The exact completed cost and terminal yield are unknown. Given stable root-choice divergence observed at one service point in only 2/8 exposed families, expected new transfer information per game is low and unquantifiable; even a favorable 18/32 development points gate would not alone estimate population Elo. By contrast, a frozen source-complete test of whether an AND/OR quiet defense or continuation response *actually changes an admissible root choice with known forced-outcome bounds*, on separately held-back motion families, has higher near-term falsifiability before a game expense. Failure to find such witnesses would itself rule out the specific mechanism under the selected horizon/budget. The full compiled-kernel route costs considerably more engineering, so it should start with an Amdahl-style inclusive cost ceiling and exact-state proof, not construction by enthusiasm. No defensible probability of success, Elo delta, token cost or full-kernel speedup can presently be calculated.

For mature precedents, [Stockfish search.cpp](https://github.com/official-stockfish/Stockfish/blob/master/src/search.cpp) combines continuation-history, pruning/reductions, retained PV and Q behavior; [PN-search](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proof-number_search) and [df-pn lambda search](https://ipsj.ixsq.nii.ac.jp/records/9787) offer alternative resource-allocation frameworks. Their chess/shogi constants and empirical results do **not** transfer without validation. Current GenericChess already implements TT/capture/promotion/killer/countermove/history, optional relative history, PVS, LMR and Q; citing a mature mechanism alone is not an implementation or gain.

They are **not** newly invented by the endpoint scripts. The [new diff](https://github.com/WD-nanophotonics/GenericChess/compare/48749ec334c9eb42955a844f06e1d101bb2a02e2...025c70cb4d35c8d2196b4a8d8dc0c9c1f2da75b8) changes research scripts, tests, reports, data and LOCAL_MAINLINE; it changes **no generic_chess production module**.

Reverse attack-source bitsets and one-ply king-safety/royal-reply geometric sufficient conditions may assist an eventual compiled kernel; movement connectivity alone gives no WDL and licenses no unsafe move pruning. The tested incremental-royal reply still falls back to existing Core/Native on unproved cases. No demonstrated whole-search end-to-end benefit from a new fused pure-Leaper kernel exists; any proposal must include Python↔Native import, semantic decoding, check/availability, action construction, exact history/TT/clock and controller lifecycle.

Representation proof has three different targets: game/action/history isomorphism, evaluator-score/argmax behavior, and legal cache lifetime. At 1 s, the only two stable changed root choices have 29/32 and 36/3 immediate legal opponent replies respectively across OFF/ON; AUX replay finds **zero immediate opponent wins in all 100 replies**, full later WDL UNKNOWN, so neither returned score nor reply count proves the choice superior.

The older PVS 5W/15D/4L across 6 families (one positive family), check extension 1W/24D/5L across 8 (none positive), and LMR 16 draws remain limited negative or inconclusive samples, not hidden independent transfer wins.

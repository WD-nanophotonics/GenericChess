## B2 — research direction and opportunity cost (priority formed from user goal before inspecting PRIMARY's current Main)

The user's desired outcome is **large transferable search/whole-service improvements and playable strength across distinct, exact pure-Leaper games**. The most valuable immediate question is whether a *rule-independent allocation of search effort* yields better retained, legal decisions and game results at **matched full-service time** across unseen, executable motion families. Candidate mechanisms include context-conditioned move history / continuation responses or bounded threat/proof extensions beyond existing noisy Q; these must be judged against the current history/countermove, PVS/LMR and Q infrastructure, not sold as new solely because orthodox Stockfish uses them. A genuinely new execution nucleus is another possible route **only** when it beats the existing compiled tables + Core/Native over the inclusive query. Existing geometric symmetry, reverse sources and component structure may generate features and safe compiled lookup keys; they cannot certify game value or justify unsafe pruning.

Comparison with mature references: [Stockfish search.cpp](https://github.com/official-stockfish/Stockfish/blob/master/src/search.cpp) uses context-sensitive history and depth/reduction policies in a mature engine; [Fairy-Stockfish](https://github.com/fairy-stockfish/Fairy-Stockfish) makes rule features reusable across variants. These are engineering precedents, **not evidence their parameters or policies transfer to arbitrary GenericChess Leapers**. The user's mathematical compilation/Leaper simplification proposal predates this patrol.

**Past two meaningful intervals and today's change.** PRIMARY's most recent meaningful research HEAD remains `31060392`; from that commit to today's sandbox review-only HEAD `d91928c5`, the only sandbox diff is the preceding patrol Markdown. PRIMARY had established finite static integer-mask evaluation cost reductions (pooled Root wall ratio 0.919395; cold lifecycle 0.932136; original zero-OFF-CPU group gates still INCONCLUSIVE), and then stopped the threat-ordering preflight at its first Core mate. The 64 qualification searches were **NOT_RUN**, 32 proposed games **NOT_STARTED**; 321/324 opening layouts were **metadata**, not new playable rule families. Neither phase established cross-family stronger decisions. AUX's new `670bae91→85b5b7d→5663e4e1` commits spent a separately reported ~62.93 conservative research minutes on static-mask consumer and representation scope and then began another **unfinished** research segment. This produced replay/equivalence controls, an evaluator perturbation bound and a typed repetition counterexample, but **no Leaper Player game or search-strength advance**. A postpublication AUX commit at `5663e4e1` is genuinely newer than the last patrol; counting only the earlier static study would miss it.

The most consequential new discovery is that **even apparently equivalent representations require two separate tests**. (i) Adding inert `UNUSED` to an explicitly restricted game preserves mapped legal play/history but changes default material profiles (A 1000→1393, B 1020→1420, C 436→607; one legal ongoing raw state -444→-615). AUX now adds a *dead promotion* to X: the specified 4×4 legal game never creates X, but the current conservative *legacy-reachable* normalization changes P 1000→569 and the ongoing root raw 1002→571. (ii) Conversely, relabeling one movable A as B with **identical movement and identical raw values** can change the game: on a fixed 24-ply path, fourfold repetition ends the uncolored game, while the colored game remains ongoing because its typed repetition identities do not coincide. Geometry-only / type-renaming exposure signatures therefore cannot certify independent rule families **or** legal game bisimulation; a many-to-one label quotient cannot safely merge repetition histories or TT keys. These are *source-backed finite counterexamples and narrow structural arguments*, not solved minimax values or evidence of improved Elo.

**Counterfactual required by repeated absence of positive cross-family gains:** another opening-layout/signature inventory may consume another source/validation session but can terminate with zero legal decision headroom; past 321/324 metadata feasibility does not estimate its success probability. A prospectively frozen **executable, full-history, nontrivial** set followed by one equal-service algorithmic contrast consumes more Core/Player time but will either falsify a broad search hypothesis or support matched-seat games. A cheaper complementary representation check can invalidate a supposed holdout before gaming resources are spent. Precise expected Elo, token costs and success probabilities are **not identifiable** from present outcomes. Thus **redirect PRIMARY away from open-ended layout construction and re-tuning exposed PVS/LMR/extension flags**, while retaining bounded opening/IR certification as a prerequisite and the measured mask wrapper as a closed, positive-but-inconclusive cost lead.

## A — engineering, exact semantics and hot path

Source inspection at pinned `31060392` confirms existing `CompiledRuleSet.leap_targets`, `empty_mobility`, `empty_forward_mobility`, existing Native legality/check/availability, `SearchPathRuntime` path-aware push/pop and collision-aware incremental identity/repetition accounting. Production `run_root_search` builds a runtime from the **complete history** and uses exact terminal/clock semantics, policy-aware TT and the existing abort/fallback contract. Its non-check Q branch performs stand-pat/cutoff before generating legal actions and classifies noisy continuations on demand; in-check evasion handling is distinct. Current `ordering.py` already has TT/capture/promotion/countermove/killer/history, relative-history and opt-in quiet-geometry facilities. Present work is not a blank-slate leap-target compiler, bitmap, or all-new Native kernel.

The PRIMARY ordinary-Leap predicate improves admission by checking both actual target-empty/target-enemy IR, owner frames, clock and initial history, beyond the older *two-cell hash footprint*. Its guarantee is only a sufficient **one-ply legal royal reply** within a restricted domain, not a general king-safety or terminal shortcut. AUX's new source examples emphasize why a compact geometric encoding must preserve *typed* repetition identity, setup inventory, promotion feasibility and every terminal/history observation before using a mathematical quotient. The full Semantic board diff, object lifetime, validation/exception sequence, Python↔Native conversion and Core terminal authority remain outside an unproved dedicated nucleus. No new integrated fused Native query-cost benchmark exists; microbenchmarks or a partial evaluator saving cannot establish a whole-search speedup. No production source/default changed in the reviewed new diffs.
